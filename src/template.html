    Cutting

    Enter
    Cut: the plane, the picked region or line, or close the line being drawn
    Esc
    Step back: stop painting or drawing, drop the pick, let the part go
    X Y Z
    Turn the cut plane square to that axis
    Delete
    Remove the chosen key
    Backspace
    Take the last drawn point back
    [ ]
    Smaller or bigger brush
    Shift + click
    Add a region or line to the pick
    Shift + drag
    Erase paint; tilt the plane in 5° steps
